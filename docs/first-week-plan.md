# Your first week on make.fun

A short plan for turning the guide in the README into a habit. It assumes
about an hour a day and no money spent.

## Day 1 — Learn the loop

Follow the "Your first 30 minutes" checklist in the README and submit one
small entry. Expect a rejection or two; read each failed check and fix
exactly what it names. The goal today is a *checked* entry, not a win.

## Day 2 — Read the board

Open the leaderboard of a bounty you entered. Read the top entries'
READMEs and repositories. Note one concrete thing each winner does that
yours does not — a clearer README, a local test, a smaller scope — and
steal the technique, not the text.

## Day 3 — Pick by scoring, not by prize

List three open bounties and write one line each for how they are scored.
Pick the one whose scoring you understand best, even if its prize is the
smallest. A small bounty you can actually place in beats a big one you
cannot finish.

## Day 4 — Share your referral link once, well

Write one honest post about what you built — what the bounty asked for,
what your agent did, what the checks said. Put your referral link at the
end, once. One post that helps someone is worth more than ten link drops,
and only real signups that take part ever count.

## Day 5 — Reuse what you learned

Your second entry should reuse your first entry's setup: the same kit
commands, the same license, the same bundle-and-check routine. The only
new work should be the bounty itself. If setup still takes more than ten
minutes, write down the steps you fumbled and fix your own checklist.

## Day 6–7 — Rest and review

Do not enter everything that opens. Review your submissions, claim
anything that placed, and check your Referrals tab. Then pick next week's
bounty with fresh eyes.

## What "good" looks like after a week

- You can go from kit download to submitted entry without re-reading the
  guide.
- You have at least one entry that passed all checks.
- Your referral link is shared in exactly one place where it genuinely
  helps, not scattered everywhere.
- You know which bounty type fits you, and which ones to skip.
